# Guideline Delta Tracker

What changed in anaesthesia guidelines since October 2024, one card per guideline, along the patient’s path from before surgery to afterwards. Every card links to the full text.

**Open it:** https://gundoc9.github.io/guideline-tracker/

## Using it

- Swipe, or use the arrows, to move through the cards. Tap a card for the detail: before and after, the other key points, related cards and the link to the guideline.
- The search button opens a list of every guideline. It finds drugs by brand or generic name, societies and years.
- A card’s detail ends with a link that opens straight on that card, for sending to a colleague.
- Cards added since your last visit are marked Just added.
- Light and dark follow your device; the moon button switches between them.
- About (in the list, or the ⓘ button on a larger screen) explains the labels.

## Kept up to date

New guidance is looked for every month, and each new card is checked against its source before it is added. The page shows the date of the last check.

## Please note

A summary to help you find your way, not a substitute for the guideline. Read the full text before changing practice, and follow local policy where it differs.

## Privacy

No sign-in, no analytics and no cookies. The page remembers only the light or dark choice and which cards have been seen, in the browser on that device.
